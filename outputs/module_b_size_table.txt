Best on this footage: orthographic (mean abs err 2.15 cm), tied exactly with full_perspective. The camera looks almost straight down at a belt that only spans a small fraction of its working distance, so the scene is close to orthographic to begin with -- the perspective-aware correction has very little foreshortening left to remove, and full_perspective's plane-fit collapses to the same answer as the plain scale transfer. affine is consistently the worst: its unconstrained shear term amplifies whatever motion-blur/shadow elongation is in the detected footprint quad (frame 135's own detected aspect ratio is 1.8:1, against a true 1.32:1 -- the detector's silhouette, not the size model, is the dominant source of error here) instead of averaging it out the way the isotropic orthographic fit does.
